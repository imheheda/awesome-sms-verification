## What is SMS verification?

Online SMS verification — also known as receiving SMS online, using a *virtual number*, or *temporary phone number* — lets you receive a one-time verification code **without using your personal SIM card**. You rent a real carrier number for a short window: long enough to receive the SMS and finish a signup or two-factor authentication (2FA) step, then it is released.

It is commonly used for:

- Signing up to apps that require a phone number (Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok…)
- Receiving 2FA / OTP codes for crypto exchanges, email and other accounts
- Keeping work, testing and personal accounts on separate numbers
- Protecting your privacy by not linking every registration to one identity

## FAQ

**Is receiving an SMS code online legal?**
Receiving a verification SMS is legal in virtually every country — you are renting the short-term use of a real number. Whether you may open multiple accounts on a given platform is governed by that platform's own terms, not by the number itself.

**How long do I keep the number?**
Typically a single SMS order is active for ~15 minutes. On [jiema.my](https://jiema.my/en), if no code arrives in that window the order auto-cancels and your balance is refunded.

**Can I reuse the same number?**
No. Once a number verifies an account on a service, that service marks it as used. Each verification needs a fresh number.
